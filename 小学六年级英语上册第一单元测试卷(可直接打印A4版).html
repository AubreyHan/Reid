Last July, Lin Tao had a wonderful and meaningful summer holiday. He went to Qingdao with his parents by train. They stayed at his uncle's house for eight days.

Emma's Summer Camp Diary

This August, Emma and her classmates went to a Nature Summer Camp in the countryside near Beijing. The camp was about forty kilometres from their school, so they went there by bus. They stayed there from the 12th to the 18th of August.

Every morning, the children got up early and fed the baby chicks, ducks and rabbits on the farm. After breakfast, they learned to water the vegetables and pick fresh tomatoes. In the daytime, they also climbed a small mountain and learned about different plants from a science teacher.

In the evening, they did not watch TV or play on phones. Instead, they sat around a campfire, sang English songs, danced and shared interesting stories. Before they left the camp, Emma made a picture book about her camp life with her team.

"I learned a lot from nature and made many new friends. It was hard to say goodbye!" Emma wrote in her diary.
